## Extension & Outreach Partners

SmartGrow works closely with:

- **[JMU Youth Programs](https://www.jmu.edu/pce/programs/all/youth-programs/index.shtml)** — A component of JMU's School of Professional and Continuing Education providing educational opportunities to K–12th grade students that inspire curiosity, growth, and success. SmartGrow delivers the SGC through this established youth network.
- **Middle School Educators** — Piloting and refining curriculum modules in real classrooms
- **Agricultural Technology Partners** — Providing equipment, expertise, and real-world context
